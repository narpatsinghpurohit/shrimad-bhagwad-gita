Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 30, 2025

This app works completely offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email, location, or usage data.

The app does not use any third-party services, trackers, or analytics tools. You can use the app freely without any external interference.

The app may request minimal permissions solely to ensure smooth functionality (such as storage access for offline use). No data is uploaded or shared.

This app is completely free of advertisements. We aim to offer a peaceful and distraction-free spiritual reading experience.

5. Children’s Privacy

The app is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk to children’s privacy.

6. Updates to This Policy

Any future updates to this privacy policy will be clearly posted within the app. You are encouraged to review this page periodically for changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us through the app or our official support channels.
